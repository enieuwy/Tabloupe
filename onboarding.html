Welcome to Tabloupe

A lens is a saved view of your tab groups. Switching one expands the groups you named and collapses the rest — your tabs stay open.

Start from the groups you already have

Looking for tab groups in this window…

    This window has no tab groups yet. In Firefox, right-click a tab and choose Add tab to new group — then come back and save that set as a lens.

    Prefer a shortcut? Open the Tabloupe toolbar popup and use Organize tabs… to cluster your open tabs into groups first.

    Two things worth knowing

    • Nothing is ever closed. Switching a lens only collapses and expands groups. Show all groups in the popup brings everything back.
    • Search every open tab with Ctrl + S on any web page — then group, close, or move the results in bulk.
